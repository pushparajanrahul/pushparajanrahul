# 🧐 About Me

👋 Hi there! I'm Rahul, a Machine Learning Engineer who trying to develop my talent on the ML domain 🧐. I mean yeah, I am trying to satisfy my hunger of knowing the world of weights and biases. But before, I am a singer, a code enthusiast, an Automation and Robotics guy, but easily overwhelmed by all the ML things I come across daily, yet(this is the most important part) trying to not sleep while reading it. Richard Dawkins, Carl Sagan, ND Tyson, S Harris, YN Harari are few exceptional individuals I like. 

I graduated with an MS in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from Arizona State University, where I worked under Prof. Farhad Ameri, Prof Hynwoong Ko at the [Semantic Computing Lab](https://labs.engineering.asu.edu/semantics). My research focused on end-to-end **Developing a Knowledge Graph Database identifying the Process and Product capabilities of Manufacturers across the USA**, specifically on **developing Auto Image Labelling pipeline using VLM's like Llama 3.2 11B instruct and OpenAI GPT-4o-mini and curating a high wuality Graph database leveraring Neo4j** as my Applied project.

Previously, I wokred as the Automation Lab Assistant with Professor Jerry Gintz for his Rapod Prototyping and Automation Lab at Arizona State University maintainig the installation of Indistrial Robots like the vision enabled Fanuc ER4ia, CRX-10ia, Universal Robots UR5e, integrating them with PLC systems for aeemslbly line automation prototype projects.

**I am actually making a big shift in my career, but not exactly**. So when I used to work as an Automation and Controls Engineer at [Schneider Electric](https://se.com/), I was still working with feedback systems and compensation circuits and control loops and tuning to reduce the error and bring a system to it's steady state, you see. So the great experience at Schneider, finaly tasked me to a projects where we used semi supervised systems to recommend process variables to achieve a steady state response, and I got a heck lot of impressed by this new deal, and hence, I soughted out the new challenge I was ready to learn and made this  ** BIG LEAP** to be an ML engineer. A tremendous journey so far and looking for more actually !!!

I'm always open to new opportunities and a good chat ☕. Feel free to connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pushparajanrahul/) or reach out at [pushparajan.rahul@gmail.com](mailto:pushparajan.rahul@gmail.com).
